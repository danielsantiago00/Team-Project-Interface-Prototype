      7. Application Acknowledgment and Waiver

      Per CEO requirements, this waiver is displayed on its own screen and you must scroll through the full text before the agreement control is enabled.

      Application Acknowledgment and Waiver

      By signing, I agree to the points below. UNFinished Business will review my application in return.

      Applying does not mean I get the job. Getting a job does not give me a contract. This is true for the job I want and for any other job. Handbooks, manuals, and benefit plans do not change this. Company policies and habits do not change either. I can quit at any time. The company can end my job at any time. Neither side needs to give notice or a reason. If I am hired, the company may change its benefits, rules, and steps. Some changes may cut my benefits.

      I allow the company to check everything in this application. I must give true and full facts. If I give false facts or leave out facts, I can be fired at any time. I can be fired without warning. The company may contact my schools, past bosses, and references. I can tell the company not to call a past boss. I give up any claim against the company for making these calls.

      The company has a drug and alcohol policy. It includes tests before and after I start work. I must agree to this policy to work here. I must follow it too. I must pass the tests to keep my job. I may also need to pass job-related physical exams to keep my job.

      The company may ask a reporting agency for a report about me. The report may cover my credit, character, and reputation. It may also cover my personal traits and how I live. I can ask for more details in writing. The company will give them to me, as the Fair Credit Reporting Act requires.

      My first 60 days of work are trial periods. During this time, either side can end the job at any time for any reason. This is also true after the trial period.

      UNFinished Business is an Equal Employment Opportunity Employer and complies with the Florida Civil Rights Act (Fla. Stat. § 760.10), the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), and the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). Any job-related physical or medical examination is conducted strictly after a conditional offer of employment.
